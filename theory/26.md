PART 11: SEMAPHORES — COUNTING RESOURCES
11.1 What is a Semaphore?

REAL WORLD: PARKING GARAGE

Parking garage has 50 spots.
A digital sign shows: "Available spots: 47"

Car enters:  sign decrements → 46
Car enters:  sign decrements → 45
...
Sign shows 0: all full! Cars must WAIT outside.
Car leaves:  sign increments → 1
Waiting car: enters, sign goes to 0 again.

THIS IS A SEMAPHORE!
- The count = available spots = "permits"
- acquire() = take a spot (decrement, wait if 0)
- release() = leave spot (increment, signal waiters)

MUTEX = Semaphore with count 1 (binary semaphore)
        Only 1 "permit" — only 1 thread at a time.

COUNTING SEMAPHORE:
        N permits — N threads can be "inside" simultaneously.

USES:
- Limit concurrent connections to database (connection pool)
- Rate limiting (permits per second)
- Signaling between threads (binary semaphore)
- Producer-consumer coordination
