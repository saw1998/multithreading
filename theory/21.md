PART 9: STREAM QUEUES — ARCHITECTURE DEEP DIVE
9.1 What is a Stream Queue and Why?

REAL WORLD: AMAZON WAREHOUSE

WITHOUT queues (direct coupling):
  Delivery Truck → immediately unloads to packing team
  If packing is busy: truck waits (idle)
  If no truck: packing team waits (idle)
  Tight coupling = inefficiency

WITH queues (decoupled):
  Delivery Truck → drops packages in RECEIVING AREA → drives away
  Packing Team ← picks from RECEIVING AREA at their pace
  
  Truck never waits!
  Packing team never starves!
  Buffer absorbs mismatches in rate!

IN SOFTWARE:
  Producer Thread → adds to queue → continues immediately
  Consumer Thread ← removes from queue ← processes at its pace

TYPES OF STREAM QUEUES:
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Unbounded Queue: Can grow infinitely — risk of OOM!            │
│ Bounded Queue:   Fixed max size — backpressure!                │
│ Priority Queue:  Higher priority items processed first         │
│ Delay Queue:     Items become available after a time           │
│ Ring Buffer:     Fixed-size, overwrites old (good for logging) │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
